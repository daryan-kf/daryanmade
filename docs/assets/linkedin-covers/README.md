## Upload your personal cover

1. Save either personal cover above.
2. LinkedIn → **Me → View Profile → cover camera/pencil → Upload**.
3. Keep the full image in the preview; avoid extra zoom. Click **Apply** on desktop, or **Save** if shown.
4. Check your profile in the mobile app, including the profile-photo overlap.

## Upload Sarpoli's images

1. Open **Sarpoli → admin view**.
2. Upload the square logo through the Page's logo/edit controls.
3. Click the **cover image/edit icon → Upload photo** and select the Sarpoli company banner.
4. Keep the full images in the previews, apply/save, then check the public Page on your phone.

The company cover is intentionally shallower than a personal cover. These files match LinkedIn's current recommended dimensions and are below its file-size limits. LinkedIn can adapt their display across screens, so preview them before finishing. [Personal cover specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217) · [Company image specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309) · [Edit a Company Page](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564346)

The banners were created with the built-in Imagegen tool. [Final prompts](final-prompts.md) record the personal designs and distinct Sarpoli document concept. Final dimensions were exported with macOS sips and checked along with the file sizes and visible composition.
