- `købeklar/components/LandingPage.tsx`: findings examples, `deliverables`, and English FAQ support source-linked findings, cross-document discrepancies, draft analysis, legal assessment, advisory letter and document overview.
- The 10–20 minute figure is the source site's typical **processing** time for a case. It does not include the lawyer's professional review. Large scanned cases can take longer.

## Motion

Each workflow has one bounded explanatory sequence. A source scanner connects extracted clauses to a review panel; the career story shows a reviewable wording refinement; property files resolve into cited findings as the estimated manual-preparation bar contracts. They play once when approached, at a fixed readable duration independent of scroll speed. Revisiting a section leaves the final frame in place. A deliberate Replay button can run the sequence again. Global pause, reduced motion and JavaScript failure leave a complete static composition; playback pauses while offscreen.
